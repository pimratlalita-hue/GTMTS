## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-density executive command cockpit tailored for higher education leadership, faculty senates, university boards, and institutional compliance bodies. The design aesthetic balances academic tradition with precision data density, translating centuries of scholarly governance into a modern, low-strain nocturnal environment.

### Design Movement & Influence
The system synthesizes **Deep Precision Technical Minimalism** with **Luminous Tonal Layering**. Drawing from high-performance terminal cockpits and archival leather-bound registries, the interface avoids decorative skeuomorphism while rejecting sterile flat interfaces. It emphasizes:
- **Restraint and Gravitas:** Visual priority given strictly to agenda voting states, curriculum approvals, tenure tracks, and budget resolutions.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Strict grid alignment, crisp hairline boundaries, and deliberate focal glow points rather than unfocused gradients.
- **Atmospheric Focus:** Built natively for dim boardroom projectors, late-night tenure review sessions, and executive tablets, minimizing eye fatigue during long sessions.

## Colors

The nocturnal palette relies on stepped slate-navy strata that establish clear spatial depths without losing contrast. Surface layering prevents visual flattening across complex governance dashboards.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#090d16`):** The absolute foundation. Absorptive, infinite deep navy. Used for window frame backgrounds, outer margins, and canvas backdrops.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#0f172a`):** Primary panel, sidebar, and container background.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1e293b`):** Cards, elevated tables, module sheets, and active dialogue shells.
- **Surface Level 3 (`#334155`):** Hover states, active row selections, dropdown menus, and popovers.

### Accent & Functional Accents
- **Academic Gold / Amber (`#d97706` / `#f59e0b`):** The primary brand accent denoting executive actions, presidential seals, voting quorums, tenure motions, and formal sign-offs.
- **Cognitive Cyan (`#0ea5e9` / `#38bdf8`):** The secondary signal for procedural state, live discussion links, active agenda items, and comparative metrics.
- **Scholastic Emerald (`#10b981` / `#34d399`):** Tertiary indicator reserved for approved charters, passed resolutions, accredited metrics, and positive budget variance.
- **Imperial Crimson (`#ef4444`):** Strict semantic utility reserved for vetoes, failed quorums, conflicts of interest, and compliance breaches.

### Text Contrast Hierarchy
- **Primary Text (`#f8fafc`):** High-contrast crisp off-white for headings, critical votes, legal charters, and active readings.
- **Secondary Text (`#94a3b8`):** Neutral cool slate for metadata, session notes, bylaws citations, and column titles.
- **Muted Text (`#64748b`):** Subordinate timestamps, inactive pagination, and subtle table grid guidelines.

## Typography

The typography strategy balances the humanistic authority of parliamentary record-keeping with the technical precision of governance analytics:
- **Headlines (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Modern, sharp, and confident. Its geometric clarity delivers presence to executive titles, session topics, and resolution names without the nostalgia of serif types.
- **Body (`Work Sans`):** Clean, open letterforms engineered for long reading cycles. It preserves legibility when rendering policy documentation, constitutional bylaws, and detailed committee briefs under dark mode conditions.
- **Labels & Data (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to procedural numbering, resolution tracking IDs (e.g., `RES-2025-084`), quorum counts, timestamp logs, and table metrics. Its monospaced structure prevents tabular jitter across dynamic voting updates.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **12-column fluid grid system** anchored within a maximum boundary of `1600px` for ultra-wide executive monitors, with standard gutters and responsive margins based on a `4px / 8px` structural cadence.

### Screen Adaptations & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid. Left persistent vertical spine navigation (`280px`), dynamic multi-pane splitting (e.g., 4-column document outline, 8-column resolution markup). Gutter: `24px` (`1.5rem`).
- **Tablet / Projector Mode (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Navigation folds into an icon rail (`72px`) or off-canvas drawer. Gutters collapse to `16px` (`1rem`). Split views convert into sequential tabbed cards.
- **Mobile Handheld (< 768px):** 4-column stack. Bottom-anchored actions for voting gestures. Horizontal scrolls strictly restricted to tabular amendment data with freeze-paned left identifiers. Margins: `16px` (`1rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, muddy dropshadows, which wash out dark canvas designs. Instead, the system relies on **layered luminance, sharp structural borders, and targeted glow cues**.

### Surface Strata System
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#090d16`. Flat background.
2. **Level 1 (Docked Containers & Sidebars):** `#0f172a` with a 1px right/bottom border of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`.
3. **Level 2 (Cards & Active Modules):** `#1e293b` with a 1px hairline perimeter of `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.12)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, and Tooltips):** `#1e293b` surrounded by an inner highlight line of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and an outer drop shadow of `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

### Glowing Border Accents
- **Amber Focus (Executive Action):** High-priority panels feature an outer glowing border: `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.5)` with `box-shadow: 0 0 12px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`.
- **Cyan Pulse (Active Session):** Live voting blocks feature `1px solid rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.6)` paired with an ambient perimeter glow of `0 0 16px -2px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.2)`.
- **Emerald Affirmation (Consensus Reached):** Resolution approval triggers a `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.6)` with subtle trace luminescence.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft Architectural (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry. 

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for micro elements, status badges, code chips, checkboxes, table cell selectors, and nested tabs.
- **Component Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for buttons, text input fields, dropdown trigger targets, and list cards.
- **Container Radius (`0.75rem` / `12px`):** Used for large executive dashboard cards, voting control consoles, and modal dialog windows.

Rounded pills (`9999px`) are strictly forbidden except for live record indicators, pulse dots, and avatar initials. Sharp, clean corners convey parliamentary gravitas and preserve space efficiency across complex data fields.

- **Secondary (Cyan Slate):** Background `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.1)`, text `#38bdf8`, border `1px solid rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.3)`. On hover: `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.2)`, border `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.5)`.
- **Ghost (Procedural):** Background transparent, text `#94a3b8`, border `1px solid transparent`. Hover: background `#1e293b`, text `#f8fafc`.

### Chips & Badges
- Built using `JetBrains Mono` at `label-sm` with upper-case tracking.
- **Quorum Met:** Emerald background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#34d399`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.
- **Debate Ongoing:** Cyan background `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.12)`, text `#38bdf8`, border `1px solid rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.3)`.
- **Tabled / Vetoed:** Rose background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, text `#f87171`, border `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#0f172a`, border `1px solid #334155`, text `#f8fafc`, placeholder `#64748b`. Height: `40px`, padding `0 12px`.
- **Active / Focused:** Border `1px solid #0ea5e9`, box-shadow `0 0 0 2px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.2)`. Background shifts to `#1e293b`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square with `2px` rounded edges. Background `#0f172a`, border `1px solid #475569`.
- When checked: Background `#d97706`, border `#f59e0b`, check mark icon `#090d16`.

### Cards & Modular Panels

- Card Header: Border-bottom `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`, padding `16px 20px`.
- Card Body: Padding `20px`.
